About M.I.N.E.

Practical help, clear biblical teaching, and faithful outreach — all for the glory of Jesus Christ.

Who we are

Morris International Novice Everything (M.I.N.E.) is the umbrella organization that holds both the Christian ministry side and the business consulting side.

  • MINE Ministries — Morris International Novice Everyone (Christian ministry & outreach)
  • M.I.N.E. LLC — Morris International Novice Everything (business & consulting)

M.I.N.E. was founded by Ollie Mitchell Morris (Mitchell) in Cedartown, Georgia.

Our mission: Provide practical help, clear biblical teaching, and faithful outreach.

We believe sound advice and the Gospel of Jesus Christ go together for whole-life impact.

Our Vision & Dreams

A growing network of practical help and biblical teaching that equips people for both daily life and eternal hope.

  • Ministry that multiplies disciples and supports outreach.
  • Business consulting that points people toward wisdom and integrity.
  • Homestead and property perspectives that honor stewardship.
  • Building something lasting under the Lordship of Jesus Christ.

A dream: the Morris Legacy Homestead

One of Mitchell’s dreams is to one day build the Morris Legacy Homestead — a simple family place here in Georgia devoted to stewardship of the land, hospitality, and prayer. We picture a home base where family can gather, guests can be welcomed, food can be grown, and ministry can be served from.

For now it is a dream we are placing in God’s hands, trusting Him with the timing — all for the glory of Jesus Christ.
