Morris Legacy Homestead Dream

We are believing and working toward establishing the Morris Legacy Homestead — a place of stewardship, practical living, and Kingdom impact on 40+ acres in the Horseleg Creek area of Rome, Georgia.

The vision includes:

  • An earth-sheltered / underground house designed for efficiency, protection, and long-term sustainability
  • A pond for water storage, recreation, and beauty
  • F-450 workshop for tools, vehicles, and projects
  • Family guest house for hospitality and gatherings
  • Barn and greenhouse for animals, food production, and self-reliance
  • Atmospheric water generator (AWG) as a supplemental drinking water source (modest solar-powered unit targeting ~2–4 gallons/day average, with filtration), complementing rainwater catchment, the pond, and a possible well

This homestead is intended as a practical base for family, ministry, and demonstrating wise stewardship of the land God provides — all for the glory of Jesus Christ.
